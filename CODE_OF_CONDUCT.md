# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, and protect other contributors' privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method in the GitHub profile for `jacobpowaza`. Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
